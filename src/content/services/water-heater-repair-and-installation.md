## Signs Your Water Heater Needs Attention

- **No hot water, or it runs out fast** — often a failing heating element, thermostat, or undersized tank.
- **Rusty or discolored hot water** — usually corrosion inside the tank or anode rod.
- **Popping or rumbling sounds** — sediment buildup, accelerated by Houston's hard water.
- **Water pooling around the base** — a tank leak, which almost always means replacement.
- **Higher energy bills** — an aging or inefficient unit working harder than it should.

If you're seeing any of these, it's worth a professional look before a small problem becomes a cold-shower emergency.

## Tank vs. Tankless

We install both, and the right choice depends on your home and habits. **Standard tank heaters** cost less upfront and are simple to service. **Tankless heaters** deliver endless hot water, take up far less space, and last longer — a great fit for larger Houston households or anyone tired of running out. We'll walk you through the trade-offs honestly so you choose what fits your home and budget.
