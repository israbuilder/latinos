A slow drain is annoying. A fully clogged one — or a backed-up main line — can bring your whole Houston home to a stop. Latinos Plumbing clears clogs quickly and, just as importantly, finds out *why* they happened so they don't keep coming back.

## Drains We Clear

- **Kitchen sinks** — grease, food, and soap buildup.
- **Bathroom sinks, tubs, and showers** — hair and soap scum.
- **Toilets** — repeated clogs that a plunger won't fix.
- **Floor and laundry drains** — lint, sediment, and debris.
- **Main sewer lines** — roots, collapse, and deep blockages affecting the whole house.

## How We Do It Right

Snaking a drain clears the immediate blockage, but it doesn't always solve the root cause. For stubborn or recurring clogs, we use **hydro jetting** — high-pressure water that scours the full inside of the pipe, removing grease, scale, and tree roots. And when a clog keeps returning, we run a **video camera inspection** to see exactly what's going on inside your line, so you're not paying to guess.

## Why Clogs Come Back in Houston

Older Houston neighborhoods often have mature trees whose roots invade sewer lines, plus decades-old cast iron and clay pipes that catch debris. Identifying these underlying issues is the difference between a temporary fix and a real solution — and it's where our local experience pays off.
