- **Burst or leaking pipes** flooding your home

## What to Do Right Now

1. **Shut off the water.** Use the fixture's shutoff valve, or your home's main shutoff for a burst pipe or major leak.
2. **Kill power** to the area if water is near electrical outlets or your water heater.
