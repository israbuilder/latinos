When a home's plumbing reaches the end of its life, chasing one leak after another stops making sense. Repiping replaces your aging, failing pipes with durable modern materials in one planned project — so you stop patching and start trusting your water again. Latinos Plumbing has repiped Houston homes for over 20 years.

## Signs It's Time to Repipe

- **Recurring leaks** in different spots — a sign the whole system is failing, not just one pipe
- **Rusty or discolored water**, especially first thing in the morning
- **Low water pressure** throughout the house
- **Visible corrosion** on exposed pipes
- **Original galvanized plumbing** common in older Houston homes

## Why Whole-Home Repiping Pays Off

Spot repairs make sense for a single failure. But when pipes are corroding system-wide, repairing them one at a time costs more over time — and you're never sure where the next leak will appear. Repiping solves it once: better water quality, restored pressure, and an end to the leak-chasing cycle. It's also a strong selling point when it's time to list your home.

## How We Work

We plan the routing to **minimize disruption** — running new lines through walls and the attic where possible, opening as few areas as we can, and keeping water on in sections during the job. Every install is done to code by **licensed, insured Texas plumbers (MPL 41319)**, and we clean up completely when we're done.

We'll inspect your system, explain whether a full or partial repipe makes sense, and give you an honest, up-front quote.
